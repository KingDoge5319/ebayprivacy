# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 30, 2026**

### Overview

This is a privately operated application used to manage eBay seller activities. It is not available for public registration or use.

### Information Accessed

The application may access eBay account information, listings, inventory, product images, orders, transaction details, and related information necessary for its operation.

### Use of Information

Information is used for listing management, image uploads, inventory synchronization, order processing, recordkeeping, and related operational activities.

Information may be stored on operator-controlled systems and exchanged with eBay or other services necessary for these functions. Personal information is not sold or used for third-party advertising.

### Security and Retention

Access to the application is restricted to its operator. Information is retained only as necessary for operational, recordkeeping, and applicable legal purposes, and is deleted when no longer required.

### Privacy Inquiries

Questions, access requests, corrections, or privacy concerns may be directed to the application operator
